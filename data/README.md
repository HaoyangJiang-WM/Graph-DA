# Included demonstration data

`argens-2010-osse-0-60min.npz` is a compact visualization bundle, not the raw
French benchmark. It contains normalized graph geometry, control-volume areas,
held-out simulator depths, eight-member NoDA and ETKF depth ensembles, and three
simulated change-SAR acquisitions at 0, 30 and 60 minutes.

The bundle is derived from the open French Mediterranean historical-flood
benchmark by Godet et al. (2025), DOI
[`10.57745/IXXNAY`](https://doi.org/10.57745/IXXNAY), distributed under the
Etalab Open Licence 2.0. The original dataset provides 5 m terrain, river
networks, 15-minute reach/lateral hydrographs, Floodos products, high-water
marks and observed peak extents. Those raw files are not redistributed here.

Important scope: hydraulics and SAR in this compact bundle are simulated. The
terrain and river geometry are event-based real-world inputs; the bundle is not
a real-SAR assimilation result.
